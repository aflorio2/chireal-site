## Research interests

Marta Memon is a PhD student in the $\left\lvert\chi\right\rangle$real (Quantum Information and Real-time evolution in QFT) Emmy Noether research group at Bielefeld University.
Her research focuses on exploring strongly correlated phases of matter through quantum correlations. Her interests include quantum field theory, QFT on curved spacetime, and differential geometry.
